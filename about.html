About this project

Why we chose to celebrate Iran's architectural treasures.

Purpose

Appreciating Persian heritage

Iran's architecture tells a story that spans more than two and a half millennia — from desert towers that cool homes without electricity, to tiled mosques whose blue domes shimmer under the sun. This website is our small way of appreciating that heritage and sharing it with classmates and visitors.

Each landmark page is kept simple on purpose: the history of the place, what makes it special, and a few key facts a student or traveller should know.

Scope

What is included — and what is not

Iran is home to more than 20 UNESCO World Heritage Sites. This project focuses on five of the most well-known locations, so that each one can be given the space and detail it deserves.

  • Persepolis — listed since 1979
  • Naqsh-e Jahan Square, Isfahan
  • Eram Garden and the tombs of Hafez and Saadi, Shiraz
  • Golestan Palace, Tehran — listed in 2013
  • The historic city of Yazd — listed in 2017

The site is built from plain HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript — no frameworks, no backend — so it works completely offline and is easy to read, modify and present in class.
